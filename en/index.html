Your parking spot number, right when you come back.

ParkNum is a simple iPhone app for saving the number of your bike or car parking spot. Type the number and save. Turn on “Notify on return,” and ParkNum shows the number in a notification when you get back near the place you saved it.

What it does

  • Type a number and saveThe number pad is ready as soon as the app opens. Enter up to 6 digits and tap Save.
  • When you parked and how long agoShows the date and time you saved the spot and how much time has passed since then.
  • Notify on return (optional)When you save, ParkNum sets up an area of about 150 m (500 ft) around your current location. When you come back into it, you get a notification such as “Your parked spot: 123.” No need to open the app.
  • WidgetsShow the number and elapsed time on your Home Screen (small and medium) and Lock Screen.
  • Status screenCheck permission settings and the accuracy of the saved location, view the saved spot on a map, and send a test notification to find out why a notification didn’t arrive.
  • Stays on your iPhoneNo account needed. Your data is stored only on your iPhone and is never sent to the developer or anyone else. No ads, no analytics.
