Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in ParkNum (“the app”), an iPhone app provided by Kenji Tayama (“the developer”).

Summary

The app does not collect any personal data. Everything the app handles is processed and stored only on your iPhone and is never sent to the developer or any third party. The app has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Information processed on your device

To provide its features, the app stores the following on your iPhone. Only the app and its widgets read it.

  • The parking spot number you enter and the date and time you saved it
  • Whether “Notify on return” is on or off
  • If you save with “Notify on return” on: your location at the time you saved (latitude and longitude) and its accuracy, the time a return was last detected, and an error description if setup failed

Tapping Clear deletes the number, the time, the saved location, and the detection history. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

If you use iOS backups such as iCloud Backup, Apple may include this data in your own backup. The developer cannot see your backups.

Permissions and why they are used

Location (While Using and Always)

Used only when you save a spot with “Notify on return” turned on. The app gets your current location once at that moment and registers an area of about 150 m (500 ft) around it with iOS region monitoring. “Always” access lets iOS detect that you have returned to this area while the app is closed. The app does not continuously record where you go, and your location never leaves your iPhone.

Notifications

Used to show your spot number when you return to the saved place, and to tell you whether the return notification was set up successfully. All notifications are local notifications created on your iPhone; no push notification server is used.

Both permissions are optional. You can save numbers and use the widgets without them. You can change permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Sharing

The developer does not receive your information, so it is never sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.

Only if you tap “Check saved spot on map” on the Status screen are the saved coordinates passed to Apple Maps (the app or the website) to show the spot on a map. Apple’s privacy policy applies to that use.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will be revised. Significant changes will be noted on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so please don’t include personal information.
