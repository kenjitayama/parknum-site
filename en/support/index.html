Support

How to use ParkNum and answers to common questions. If this page doesn’t solve your problem, please contact us using the form.

How to use

  1. Open the app. The number pad appears. Enter your parking spot number (up to 6 digits).
  2. If you want a notification when you come back, turn on “Notify on return.”
  3. Tap Save. ParkNum shows the number, when you saved it, and how much time has passed.
  4. When you pick up your car or bike, tap Clear. This deletes the number and the saved location and stops the return notification.

FAQ

Why does ParkNum need “Always” location access?

“Notify on return” works by having iOS detect when you come back within about 150 m (500 ft) of the place you saved, even while ParkNum is closed. iOS can only do this in the background if Location is set to “Always.”

ParkNum gets your current location only once, when you tap Save. After that, iOS watches for your arrival; ParkNum does not record where you go. Your location is used only on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere.

If you don’t use “Notify on return,” you don’t need to allow location access at all. Saving numbers and the widgets work without it.

How do I change Location to “Always”?

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Apps → ParkNum → Location (on some iOS versions: Settings → ParkNum → Location).
  3. Choose “Always.” We also recommend turning on “Precise Location.”

You can also jump to Settings from ParkNum’s Status screen (the ⓘ button at the top right of the number screen) or from “Open Settings” in the notice on the number screen.

What happens if I keep “While Using the App”?

You can still save numbers, but you won’t get the return notification while the app is closed. The number screen shows a notice: “Background notifications need Always Allow.”

I came back but didn’t get a notification

First, open the Status screen with the ⓘ button at the top right of the number screen and check what it shows. Then check the following:

  • Did you leave and then come back? The notification is sent when you move from outside the area (about 150 m around the saved spot) to inside it. You won’t get one while you stay near the spot.
  • Is Location set to “Always”? On the Status screen, Location should show “Always.”
  • Are notifications allowed? In Settings → Notifications → ParkNum, make sure Allow Notifications is on.
  • Is a Focus on? Make sure a Focus such as Sleep or Do Not Disturb isn’t silencing notifications.
  • Is the saved location right? On the Status screen, tap “Check saved spot on map” and confirm the pin is where you actually parked.
  • Is the saved location accuracy “Low”? Accuracy can drop underground or inside buildings. Try saving again in a more open area.
  • Do notifications arrive at all? Tap “Send a test notification” on the Status screen. A test notification should arrive in about 3 seconds.

iOS decides when to report your arrival, so depending on signal conditions the notification may be a few minutes late.

I got “Couldn’t set up the return notification”

When you saved, ParkNum couldn’t get your current location or register the return area, or location or notification access isn’t allowed. The notification text explains the reason. Check the permissions, tap Clear, and save again. When setup succeeds, you’ll get “Return notification is ready.”

How do I stop the return notification?

Tap Clear. This removes the return area, and no more notifications will be sent. If you don’t want a notification next time, turn off “Notify on return” before you tap Save. Until you clear it, you may get a notification each time you leave the area and come back.

How do I add a widget?

  • Home Screen: Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose ParkNum (small or medium).
  • Lock Screen: Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen, then add ParkNum in the widget area.

The widgets show the number, when you saved it, and the elapsed time.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. ParkNum has no accounts and no servers. Deleting the app deletes its data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use ParkNum on iPad?

ParkNum is made for iPhone.

Contact

Please send bug reports, feature requests, and questions through the contact form. No account or sign-in is needed.

Open the contact form

Along with your message, the form asks for your iOS version, device (for example, iPhone 15), app version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Filling in what you can helps us find the cause. You can find the app version in Settings → General → iPhone Storage → ParkNum (the app list may take a few seconds to appear).

Your email address is optional. Enter it only if you’d like a reply. For how we handle information sent through the form, see the Privacy Policy.

Known issues and requests

Reported bugs and feature requests, and their status, are listed publicly on GitHub Issues. Anyone can view them. Posting there requires a GitHub account; if you have one, you’re also welcome to open an issue directly.

Reports sent through the form may be summarized on Issues without any personally identifiable information.

GitHub Issues are public. Please don’t include personal information such as your address, exact location, or email address.
